These principles were shaped by our team and reflect how we want to work — with empathy, clarity, and a shared commitment to better outcomes.

They guide us in creating accessible, intuitive, and meaningful experiences that serve real people and reflect our values. These aren't just words — they're reminders of how we show we care, collaborate, and continually improve together.

## 1. Design with empathy

_👑 Top principle, bold and human_

We put people first. Every decision starts with understanding the needs, goals, and realities of those who use our products.

## 2. Make it simple and usable

Clear, focused design helps people get things done — fast. We eliminate clutter and prioritise ease.

## 3. Be inclusive by default

We design for everyone. Accessibility, clarity, and flexibility are built in — not added later.

## 4. Build with consistency

Shared styles and patterns create trust, speed up delivery, and reduce rework. Familiarity matters.

## 5. Collaborate openly

Apiary is built by many. We share ideas early, document clearly, and work together to improve every step.

## 6. Evolve continuously

Design is never done. We test, learn, and adapt — always with the goal of doing better.

> These principles are living, not static — and they reflect what our team believes matters most. We'll revisit and evolve them as we grow.
