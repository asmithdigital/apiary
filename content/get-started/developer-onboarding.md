This page guides developers working on RAA's digital products to use Apiary efficiently. Apiary provides a shared system of design foundations, components, and documentation that promotes consistency, quality, and faster delivery.

## How designs are shared

Designers share visual specifications via Figma, usually linked in Jira tickets. You can inspect styles and tokens directly in Figma Dev Mode for precise implementation details. Apiary's documentation complements this by providing guidance and standards.

## The role of Storybook

Storybook will become a key tool to speed up development. It will provide an interactive library of coded components, helping you:

- Quickly find existing components ready for use
- Access live examples and detailed specs
- Review guidelines for new or updated features before coding

_Together, Figma and Storybook will streamline your workflow and reduce rework._

## Why use Apiary?

- Build faster with reusable, well-documented components
- Maintain consistency across teams and platforms
- Ensure accessibility is built in from the start
- Reduce back-and-forth with designers and other stakeholders

## How you can help

- Report issues or gaps you find
- Suggest improvements or new components
- Collaborate closely with designers during development

> 💬 Questions or ideas? Reach out to the Apiary team or your squad designer anytime.
