## Accessibility

- To meet WCAG 2.2, the helper icon should include descriptive alt text (e.g. "Support icon with smiling face and headset"), have sufficient contrast, and not rely on visuals alone to convey meaning
- Use semantic elements (e.g. &lt;div role="button"&gt; or &lt;a&gt;) with proper ARIA labels
- Fully keyboard navigable — Tab to focus, Enter/Space to activate, Esc to close
- Use aria-live for updates and aria-current="page" for active help topics
- Don't rely on colour alone — add icons or text

## Content guidelines

**Be Friendly and Approachable** — Use a warm, conversational tone. Avoid overly formal or robotic language. e.g. "Hi! How can I help you today?" not "Please state your inquiry."

**Be Context-Aware** — Tailor responses to the page or task the user is currently working on.

**Guide, Don't Overwhelm** — Break complex processes into small steps. Offer next-step suggestions and links to related help articles.

**Respect User Attention** — Don't interrupt unnecessarily — offer help subtly, waiting for interaction rather than a demanding pop-up.

**Ensure Accessibility** — All helper text should be screen reader compatible and keyboard navigable. Don't rely on colour alone for meaning.
