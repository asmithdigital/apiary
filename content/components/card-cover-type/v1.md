Cards display content and actions about a single subject — grouping related information so users can quickly scan and compare options.

## Anatomy

- Approximate mapping — no exact real capture of a generic "Basic Card" exists yet. Basic Card anatomy per the source: Heading, Content Bundle (icon, label, paragraph, link label), optional Button Section, optional Image placeholder.

## Transitions

- Container transform pattern to reveal additional content when expanded — cards don't flip

## Accessibility

- Cards can be non-actionable containers that include buttons or links, or directly actionable without containing other interactive elements — avoid stacking interactions
- Tab: directly actionable cards move to the next card; non-actionable cards with buttons/links move to the next actionable element
- Space or Enter confirms the focused action

## Content guidelines

**Accommodate content, don't constrain it** — Let card height grow naturally with the text rather than forcing fixed-size consistency.

**Avoid truncating important content with ellipses** — Truncation makes content harder to understand and inaccessible to screen readers.
