A popup menu is a temporary, contextual menu that appears near a triggering element to show related actions. It should be concise, easy to navigate with keyboard, and dismissible by clicking outside or pressing Esc. Use smooth transitions for a polished experience.

## Anatomy

- A popup menu includes a trigger (icon or button), a container, and a list of menu items. Items may have text, icons, or shortcuts, positioned near the trigger with clear boundaries and states for hover, focus, and active.
- 1. Title/Header, 2. Popup container bubble point, 3. Close Icon, 4. Title, 5. Description, 6. Icon, 7. Popup/Modal Container

## Typography

- Popup title/header — Bold/16px | 22px
- Sub-header icon — 32×32px
- Sub-header label — 16px | 22px
- Sub-header description — 14px | 20px

## Behaviour

- Opens as a modal with a fade-and-slide-up animation, 200–300ms, ease-out. Closes with a fade-and-slide-down animation, 150–250ms, ease-in. All transitions must be interruptible and accessible.

## Types

**Support contact modal** — A centred overlay providing immediate access to help options (e.g. live chat, phone support), typically triggered by a "Help"/"Contact Us" button, temporarily blocking background interaction.

## Accessibility

- Triggered as a modal dialog that traps keyboard focus until dismissed, preventing background interaction
- The heading is a semantic heading (e.g. <h2>) for screen reader context
- Interactive options are focusable buttons/links with clear, descriptive labels
- The close button is keyboard-accessible with an aria-label (e.g. "Close popup")
- Icons are decorative or carry aria-hidden="true" where appropriate; essential info (like hours) is plain text

## Do

- Include a clear, descriptive heading that communicates the popup's purpose
- Make interactive elements easily navigable by keyboard and screen reader, with proper ARIA roles/labels
- Trap focus within the popup while it's open
- Provide a visible, clearly labelled close button
- Ensure high contrast, readable text, and support for text resizing

## Don't

- Rely on icons alone — pair with supporting text or accessible labels
- Prevent users from closing the popup or navigating without a mouse
- Overload the popup with too much information
- Allow interaction with background content while the popup is active
- Assume all users share the same input methods or abilities

## Content guidelines

**Be clear and concise** — Plain language, short sentences, key information first.

**Use clear headings** — A friendly, helpful heading that sets context, e.g. "Got a question? We can help".

**Guide the user** — Clear actions with short, direct labels and supporting text explaining what happens next.

**Respect tone and brand voice** — Friendly, professional, reassuring — especially during problem-solving.

**Prioritise important information** — Contact details, hours, or help options in a logical order; bold key parts like phone numbers.

**Support accessibility** — Avoid overly complex language; add aria-labels where needed; don't embed text in images.

**Keep it focused** — One clear purpose per popup — don't overload with marketing or unrelated content.
