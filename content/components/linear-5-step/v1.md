A stepper guides users through a multi-step process in an organised and intuitive manner, providing clarity and reducing cognitive load, letting them focus on one task at a time. It offers visual feedback on progress and lets users revisit prior steps if adjustments are needed.

## Anatomy

- A step number and title, plus a progress track. The track's state aligns to the step number/title's state below it. The step number changes to a check icon once complete.
- 1. Step number, 2. Step title, 3. Progress Track

## Size

- Space between step and track — 4px
- Space between step number and title — 6px

## Typography

- Complete step number — Font Awesome 6 Pro/14px | 20px
- Complete step title — Medium/14px | 20px
- Active step number/title — Bold/14px | 20px
- Default step number — Bold/14px | 20px
- Default step title — Medium/14px | 20px

## Behaviour

- The active state's step number has a slight animated outer glow

## Accessibility

- Use &lt;nav&gt; or &lt;ol&gt; to group the stepper, &lt;li&gt; for individual steps
- Each step needs a clear, descriptive label conveying its purpose
- Use aria-current="step" for the current step
- Use aria-labelledby/aria-label to associate each step with its description
- Consider role="progressbar" if the stepper reflects progress quantitatively
- Keyboard-navigable (Tab, Shift+Tab)
- Announce status updates (e.g. "Step 2 of 5 completed") via aria-live

<div class="apy-do">

**Do**

- Ensure the step corresponds to the page title/step subtitle
- Visually highlight the current step
- Implement ARIA attributes and full keyboard/screen reader navigation
- Permit navigating back to previous steps unless there's a specific reason not to
- Limit the number of steps to what's necessary

</div>

<div class="apy-dont">

**Don't**

- Add unnecessary steps or overly complex instructions
- Ignore loading states — show a visual indicator if a step involves loading time
- Block progress without feedback on what needs correcting
- Assume users will remember details from previous steps without a summary or review option

</div>
