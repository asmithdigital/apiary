The information drawer is a flyout panel that opens when a user clicks a link, providing additional contextual information to assist with completing a task.

## Anatomy

- A background container with a drop shadow
- A close button
- A title/heading(s)
- Body copy — can contain lists, links & subheadings

## Size

- 24px padding in the container
- 24px gap between elements

## Behaviour

- Animates in and out from the right side of the page
- Enables scroll within the panel
- Users can click the page background or close icon to dismiss (desktop)
- Users can swipe to close (mobile)
- A blanket overlays the main site in the background while open

## Accessibility

- Minimum size for the close icon — 44×44px
- Treat as a flyout — keyboard tabbing should stay within the flyout, not the background page; avoid a keyboard trap
- Announce the open action to screen reader users
- If chat is available, ensure the drawer doesn't hide the chat button when open

## Do

- Support keyboard navigation and screen reader announcements
- Always open on the right side on desktop; animate in from the right to fill the screen on mobile
- Let users close via the close icon, Esc, clicking the background (desktop), or swipe (mobile)
- Fit the drawer height to the viewport and allow scroll for longer content

## Don't

- Use the drawer for mandatory instructions — if users must read it to proceed, put it on the main page
- Open multiple drawers at once — only one should be active at a time

## Content guidelines

**Use clear, action-driven link text** — The text that opens the drawer should say plainly what it does.

**Keep content concise and relevant** — Only what supports the field or section it's attached to.

**Use plain language** — Easy for users of all levels to understand.

**Don't overload the drawer** — It's for supplemental info, not full documentation.
