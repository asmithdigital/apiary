The informational footer used across all web pages — one of RAA's three footer types.

## Anatomy

- Link group (Privacy Policy, Cookies Policy, Terms & Conditions) with dot dividers (6px horizontal padding)
- Copyright text
- Background matches the section above: P1/100, P3/100, or S2/30
- Desktop horizontal centred; Tablet vertical stack centred; Mobile vertical stack left-aligned with a 0.8px P2/30 divider
