Cards display content and actions about a single subject. Cards are containers used to group and present related information and actions about a single subject, helping users quickly scan and compare options or content.

## Anatomy

- Promo Card, Promo Card Heading, Paragraph, Card promo image

## Size

- Padding left/right — spacing-md
- Padding top/bottom — 16px
- Gap — spacing-sm

## Transitions

- Cards use a container transform pattern to reveal additional content when expanded — cards don't flip over to reveal information

## Accessibility

- Cards can be non-actionable containers that include buttons or links, or directly actionable without containing other interactive elements — avoid stacking interactions by placing actions on an already-actionable surface
- Tab: on directly actionable cards, moves to the next card; on non-actionable cards with buttons/links, moves to the next actionable element
- Space or Enter confirms the focused action

## Content guidelines

**Accommodate content, don't constrain it** — Let card height grow naturally with the text. Prioritise clarity over fixed-size consistency.

**Avoid truncating important content with ellipses** — Truncation makes content harder to understand, inaccessible to screen readers, and inconsistent across cards of varying content lengths.
