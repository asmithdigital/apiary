This guide outlines what you need to know and do to effectively use Apiary in your design work and collaborate smoothly with your team.

Apiary supports designers by providing shared components, principles, and tools to create consistent, accessible, and high-quality user experiences. This page walks you through key steps to get started, including learning our guidelines, using the right tools, and contributing your work back to the system. Detailed guides and resources are being developed and will be added here soon.

## Learn the guidelines

- Explore Apiary's core design principles that shape our approach
- Familiarise yourself with foundational elements like colour, typography, and accessibility
- Understand the components and patterns used across web and mobile products
- Learn how to use design tokens in Figma to keep designs consistent

## Get the tools

- Use the official Figma project template to start new product designs
- Make sure you have access to the RAADS Figma design system assets
- Review the structure and correct use of design tokens in your designs
- Follow the branching and version control process when updating designs collaboratively

## Contribute to Apiary

- Share your work regularly with the design team for feedback and alignment
- Once designs are approved, create a branch in the RAADS Figma file and add your work
- Submit a merge request to the design team for review and approval
- After merging, document your changes following Apiary's documentation process
- Communicate your updates to the wider team through the designated channels
