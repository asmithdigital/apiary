A clear and consistent colour system helps us design user-friendly, accessible, and brand-aligned experiences.

Colour in Apiary supports branding, usability, and emotion — helping teams create consistent, inclusive products that scale across platforms. By using a shared colour system, we reduce duplication, improve readability, and build trust with our users.

## How we use colour

- Branding — strengthens brand recognition and visual identity through consistent use of primary and secondary colours
- Functional cues — supports usability by indicating interaction states (hover, active, disabled) and system status (error, success)
- Emotion and focus — creates a visual tone that builds trust and guides attention
- Accessibility — ensures text and interface elements meet contrast guidelines for everyone, including users with vision impairments

## Our colour system

- Primary and secondary brand colours
- Neutrals and greys for layout and background elements
- Semantic colours for messaging and status indicators
- Accessible colour pairings that meet WCAG standards

_Colour usage examples and pairings live in Figma files and component documentation._

## Using colour in your work

Always use the colours defined in the Apiary design system — not custom values — to ensure consistency across products. Designs should reference the official Figma libraries, and developers should follow specifications provided in design handovers. We're preparing our colour system to support design tokens in future stages.
